Teleport Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Teleport is a clipboard and file-transfer service operated by Anna Sidorova ("Teleport", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we process personal information when you use the Teleport apps for Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows, including versions distributed through app stores or directly.

Contact us about privacy or your information at developer@teleportclipboard.app.

Information we process

Account information. We process your email address to create your account, send sign-in codes, and authenticate you. Our authentication service also processes an account identifier, session records, sign-in timestamps, IP addresses, and technical request information such as user-agent information. Teleport does not require an account profile name or an advertising identifier.

Your devices and connections. We process an app-generated device identifier, device name, operating-system type, public cryptographic key and its fingerprint, last-seen timestamps, and network addresses and ports used to connect your devices. A default device name may come from your operating system; you can rename devices in Teleport. Network addresses can include local or overlay network addresses. We use this information to show your devices, recognize trusted peers, and establish transfers. The cryptographic fingerprint identifies a device key; it is not an advertising or browser fingerprint.

Clipboard and files. Depending on your platform, permissions, and actions, Teleport reads clipboard content and content you explicitly share or select, such as text, links, images, files, filenames, folder structure, and file metadata. This information can contain personal or sensitive information chosen by you. Teleport transfers it to other devices signed in to your account. Clipboard access and background operation vary by operating system.

Direct transfers send content between your devices over an encrypted connection. For small supported clipboard items, Teleport can use a cloud relay. The source device encrypts the relay payload for your recipient devices before uploading it. Device private keys are kept on the devices and are not uploaded to our backend. The relay stores encrypted payloads together with readable routing metadata, including account and device identifiers, content type, transfer identifier, timestamps, and recipient-key information. File contents and larger items require a direct connection; Teleport does not provide cloud file storage.

Diagnostics. Release versions send error and crash reports to Sentry to help us identify and fix problems. Reports may contain an app-generated device identifier, app version and build, operating-system or runtime information, error types, stack traces, and technical operation information. Crash reports can include bounded technical log files. Our configuration removes account email addresses, account identifiers, clipboard payloads, file paths, request bodies, and other free-text fields from diagnostic events, and masks designated sensitive values in attached logs. These measures reduce disclosure; please contact us if you believe sensitive information was included in a report. Diagnostic identifiers are not fully anonymous: a device identifier can be associated with its account. Sentry also receives network information needed to accept reports, such as the connection's IP address. We do not enable advertising tracking, session replay, or performance tracing in the apps.

Support requests. If you contact us, we process your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending passwords, sign-in codes, private keys, or unrelated clipboard or file content.

How we use information

We use this information to provide and secure your account, connect your own devices, transfer the content you choose to share, display device availability, respond to support and privacy requests, investigate errors or abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, rent it to others, use it for targeted advertising, or use your clipboard and files to train AI models. Teleport has no public feed or feature for publishing your clipboard to other users.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information necessary to provide the service you request, pursue legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the service, comply with legal obligations, or rely on consent where required. You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent by contacting us or changing the relevant device permission. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Who receives information

  • Your other devices receive content and the device information needed to recognize and connect to each other.
  • Supabase provides authentication, the account and device database, realtime delivery, and the encrypted clipboard relay. It processes account, connection, and relay information on our behalf.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails through our authentication service. It processes the destination email address, email contents, and delivery-related information. Your email provider also receives these messages.
  • Sentry processes diagnostic reports and any technical log attachments on our behalf.
  • Email and infrastructure providers process communications, network requests, and operational information needed to host and support the service.

We use service providers for the purposes described in this policy and require appropriate protection of information they process for us. Their infrastructure may be located in the United States or other countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, those safeguards must apply to the transfer. App-store operators independently process information associated with downloads, installation, and their own services under their privacy policies.

We may disclose information when legally required, to respond to valid legal process, or as reasonably necessary to protect people, our service, or legal rights. If operation of Teleport is transferred, personal information may be transferred with it, subject to applicable law and appropriate notice of material changes to how it is used.

Storage and retention

We keep account and registered-device records while your account exists, unless you remove a device or delete the account. Authentication and operational records may have separate retention needed for security and service operation.

Teleport is not a clipboard-history service. A new clipboard publication replaces the previous current relay record. The time during which an item can be exchanged is different from how long an encrypted record can remain in the database: encrypted relay data may remain until replaced or deleted. Our current Supabase plan provides a rolling seven-day window of daily database backups; copies of deleted records can remain in those backups until they age out. Operational records held by service providers have separate retention.

On-device preferences, session data, keys, caches, and temporary transfer data support app operation. Completed files and copies pasted into another app remain under your control. Ending an exchange or deleting your Teleport account does not erase another app's clipboard history, saved files, or operating-system backups. Interrupted transfers or crashes can leave local temporary data until cleanup or removal of app data.

Diagnostic reports are retained for troubleshooting according to the configured service retention period. Local diagnostic logs are bounded and rotated; the host apps normally retain at most two launch sessions, with logs older than seven days removed on a later launch. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve and document your request, handle follow-up, or meet legal obligations. We do not promise immediate erasure of every service log or backup when an account is deleted. Information that must be retained is limited to the reason for retention and protected accordingly.

Your choices and requests

You can rename or remove registered devices, control operating-system permissions, sign out, stop using the apps, and delete your account using the in-app account controls. Removing a device revokes its service sessions; it does not remotely erase copies already received by that device. Signing out is not the same as deleting an account.

Account deletion removes your authentication account and its associated live account, device, and relay records. Previously submitted diagnostic reports, support correspondence, operational logs, and backups may require separate handling as described above. Email us if you need help deleting an account or request removal of associated information outside the live account database. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify that the account or information belongs to you. Do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary and agree on a suitable method.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of your information, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Send requests to developer@teleportclipboard.app. We will handle them within the time required by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Children

Teleport is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Teleport, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it as appropriate. Where local law requires a higher age or parental authorization to use the service, that requirement applies.

Tracking signals

The Teleport apps do not perform advertising tracking across other companies' apps or websites. They do not change their behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

These information pages

Our public Teleport privacy, account-deletion, and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection and request information, such as your IP address, when it serves these pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing practices. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, or embedded third-party content to these pages.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates to this policy and revise the date above. For material changes, we will provide additional notice where required by applicable law.
