Delete your Teleport account

You can delete your Teleport account using the account controls inside the app. Choose the account-deletion action and confirm your request.

If you cannot access the app, email developer@teleportclipboard.app with the subject Teleport account deletion, preferably from the email address used for your Teleport account. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password, sign-in codes, or identity documents in your initial message.

Deleting the account removes your authentication account and associated live account, device, and encrypted relay records. It does not erase files you have saved, clipboard copies held by other apps, or your operating-system backups. Diagnostic reports, support messages, service logs, and backups may have separate retention. Mention these in your request if you want help removing associated information outside the live account database.

Request deletion of specific data

You can also request deletion of specific information without deleting your account. Email developer@teleportclipboard.app with the subject Teleport data deletion, identify the account email, and explain which information you want removed. We may need to verify account ownership. We will explain if information must be retained for security or legal reasons, or if deleting information required for account operation would prevent the service from working. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or unrelated clipboard content.

Our current database backups have a rolling seven-day window; deleted records may remain there until backups age out. Diagnostic reports, provider operational logs, and support correspondence have separate retention as described in the policy. A request can include removal of associated diagnostics or support data.

See the Teleport Privacy Policy for details about retention and your privacy rights.
